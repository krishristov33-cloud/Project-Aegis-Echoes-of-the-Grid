# Aegis spec

The full enhanced spec is being written now and will be mirrored here from the Main Brain vault (`Apex/Projects/Project Aegis.md`). Until it lands, `docs/TASKS.md` holds the slice scope, the owners and the contracts.

Approved enhancements beyond Kris's original doc:
- Sofia is the first grid node: Serdika ruins, the metro and Boyana church sit on the network; every ancient temple is linked underground to Antarctica.
- The mix is the health bar: damage clips and crushes the music, healing cleans it.
- Sidechain combat: Kael's hits duck enemies for a short punish window.
- Frame-drop blink leaves a ghost-frame decoy that enemies keep shooting.
- Tempo Sync reads the BPM of loaded music, so Kris's own tracks can drive the game.
